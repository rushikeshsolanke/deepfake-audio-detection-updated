About Deepfake Audio Detection

Our Mission

At Deepfake Audio Detection, our mission is to safeguard the integrity of digital audio content in an era where technology can be used to manipulate and deceive. We are committed to developing cutting-edge AI-powered solutions that can accurately detect and flag deepfake audio, empowering individuals and organizations to make informed decisions about the content they encounter.

Our Story

Launched in 2024 by our group from Matoshri College of Engineering and Research Centre, Nashik. The AI specialists and cybersecurity professionals, Deepfake Audio Detection was created in response to the rapid rise of powerful audio manipulation tools. As deepfake technology grew in complexity and availability, we saw the critical need for reliable detection methods to combat misinformation, fraud, and malicious use.

Over the years, we've collaborated with leading academic institutions, media organizations, and tech companies to refine our algorithms and expand our database of audio samples. Today, our platform stands as a beacon of trust in the digital audio landscape, serving clients across various sectors including journalism, law enforcement, and entertainment.
